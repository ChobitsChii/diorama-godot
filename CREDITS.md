## 🎨 3D-Design & Grafik
- **Design:** Zero-Asset Low-Poly Modulsystem (CSG- & Primitiv-Meshes in purem Godot 4)
- **Palette:** Modulon Cozy Color Palette (warmes Gras `#48bb78`, azurblaues Wasser `#3182ce`, sanfte Erdtöne)
- **Rendering:** GL Compatibility Renderer mit ACES/Filmic Tonemapping
